There have been several live-action and animated versions of Spider-Man in movies:

**Live-Action Spider-Man:**
*   **Tobey Maguire:** Portrayed Peter Parker in Sam Raimi's trilogy: *Spider-Man* (2002), *Spider-Man 2* (2004), and *Spider-Man 3* (2007).
*   **Andrew Garfield:** Played Peter Parker in *The Amazing Spider-Man* (2012) and *The Amazing Spider-Man 2* (2014).
*   **Tom Holland:** The current live-action Spider-Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, appearing in *Captain America: Civil War* (2016), *Spider-Man: Homecoming* (2017), *Avengers: Infinity War* (2018), *Avengers: Endgame* (2019), *Spider-Man: Far From Home* (2019), and *Spider-Man: No Way Home* (2021).

**Animated Spider-Man (Voice Actors):**
*   **Shameik Moore** as Miles Morales in *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* (2018) and *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* (2023).
*   **Chris Pine** as an alternate Peter Parker and **Nicolas Cage** as Spider-Man Noir in *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse*.

**Which one is loved by the public the most?**
Based on various polls and public opinion, **Tobey Maguire** is often cited as the most loved live-action Spider-Man by a significant portion of the audience. However, all three live-action actors (Maguire, Garfield, and Holland) have dedicated fanbases, and opinions can vary widely.

**Which Spider-Man movie has the coolest intro scene?**
Determining the "coolest" intro scene is subjective and can depend on individual preference. However, some intros that are often highlighted include:
*   **The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014):** Known for its dynamic and action-packed opening chase scene.
*   **Spider-Man (2002):** The original film's opening is memorable for setting the tone for the trilogy.
*   **The Amazing Spider-Man (2012):** Features an opening that delves into Peter Parker's childhood.

Many fans also praise the visually innovative and stylistic opening sequences of the *Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse* and *Across the Spider-Verse* films for their unique animation and introduction to the multiverse.
